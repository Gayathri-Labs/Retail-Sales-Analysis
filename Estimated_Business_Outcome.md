**RETAIL SALES ANALYSIS — ESTIMATED BUSINESS IMPACT**

Dataset: Auto\_Sales.csv (2,747 orders, Jan 2018 - May 2020)

=====================================================



METHOD:



\- Aggregated revenue by order status, product line, customer, country, and month.

\- Quantified revenue tied up in problematic order statuses (Cancelled,

&#x20; Disputed, On Hold) as "at-risk revenue."

\- Measured customer revenue concentration (Pareto analysis) and seasonality.



**KEY FINDINGS:**



1\. Total revenue across the dataset: $9,760,274.



2\. Order fulfillment risk: 118 orders (4.3% of all orders) are Cancelled,

&#x20;  Disputed, or stuck On Hold, representing $445,673 (4.6%) of total revenue

&#x20;  sitting at risk. Cancelled orders alone account for 60 orders / $194,485.



3\. Product concentration: Classic Cars is the top product line, generating

&#x20;  $3,842,896 — 39.4% of total revenue — from a single category.



4\. Customer concentration (Pareto effect): the top 20% of customers (18 of 89)

&#x20;  generate 41.9% of total revenue, indicating meaningful dependency on a

&#x20;  small set of key accounts.



5\. Seasonality: November is by far the strongest month, generating

&#x20;  $2,088,549 — about 21% of annual revenue in a single month — indicating a

&#x20;  clear seasonal demand spike worth planning inventory/staffing around.



6\. Geographic concentration: the USA is the top market at $3,355,590

&#x20;  (34.4% of revenue), followed by Spain and France.



7\. Discounting: large deals are discounted \~30% below MSRP on average vs.

&#x20;  \~4% premium on small deals, showing discount depth scales with deal size.





**ESTIMATED BUSINESS OUTCOME:**



\- If order-fulfillment process improvements (e.g., better inventory/credit

&#x20; checks, proactive dispute resolution) reduced the cancelled/disputed/on-hold

&#x20; rate by half, an estimated **$222,800 (2.3% of total revenue)** in currently

&#x20; at-risk orders could be recovered/converted to shipped revenue.



\- Given **41.9%** of revenue depends on just **18 accounts,** a formal key-account

&#x20; retention program covering this group directly protects roughly **$4.1M** in

&#x20; annual revenue.
